Welcome

SNAP (Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program) is a government program that helps individuals and families afford food through monthly benefits. This site provides easy access to official SNAP resources, including applications, status updates, EBT card support, and general program information in one organized place.

NYS SNAP Assistance Hub

This website is a shortcut and resource page helping New Yorkers quickly access official SNAP (Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program) resources. Users can find links to applications, card replacement info, FAQs, and more.

Features

Direct links to NYS/NYC SNAP application, status check, and help pages
A built-in survey to find out what users need most help with
Accessible, mobile-friendly design
No account required, no personal information collected beyond optional anonymous labels stored locally in the browser

Disclaimer

This site is not affiliated with NY State or NYC government. It does not process benefits or store personal information on external servers. Any survey responses are stored locally in the user's browser only.

Future integration with official SNAP systems would follow their data privacy policies. This prototype does not collect or transmit user data beyond local browser storage.
